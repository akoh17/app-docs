Text Grab

Grab any text. Copy instantly. All on your device.

Text Grab pulls text out of any image. Draw a box over exactly the text you want, and it's recognized on your iPhone and copied to your clipboard — no accounts, no servers, nothing ever leaves your device.

What it does

  • Precise selection — draw a box (or several) over exactly the text you want, instead of accepting whatever the system guesses. Pinch to zoom for small text.
  • Smart Suggestions — likely-wanted values are detected automatically: tracking numbers, 2FA codes, Wi-Fi passwords, links, emails, phone numbers, and QR/barcodes. Tap to copy — or to open, call, or email.
  • Card cert lookup — snap a PSA, CGC or TAG slab and go straight to its certification page.
  • Shoot or import — take a photo, or pick any photo or screenshot from your library.
  • History — every extraction is saved and re-copyable.
  • Shortcuts & Siri — automate text extraction with the "Extract Text from Image" action.

Free and Pro

Text Grab is free to use: draw a box over any text and copy it as often as you like, from the camera or the photo picker — plus automatic detection of links, email addresses and phone numbers, and your last 10 extractions.

Your first 20 Pro actions are free, counted in uses rather than days, so nothing expires while the app sits unopened. After that, Text Grab Pro is one lifetime payment of $2.99 — no subscription, no account — and unlocks tracking numbers, 2FA codes, Wi-Fi passwords, receipt totals and QR/barcodes; multi-select; card cert lookup; unlimited, searchable history; Shortcuts and Siri; and per-type control over which suggestions appear.
