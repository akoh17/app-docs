Privacy Policy

Text Grab · Last updated: 17 October 2026

Text Grab is built to work entirely on your device. It does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, and it has no servers, accounts, analytics, tracking, or advertising.

What the app accesses

  • Camera — only when you choose to take a photo. The image is processed on your device and is never uploaded.
  • Photos — only the specific images you pick. Text Grab uses Apple's system photo picker, which shares just your chosen image and nothing else about your library.

All text recognition is performed locally using Apple's on-device Vision framework. No image ever leaves your iPhone, and recognized text leaves it only when you look up a graded card (below).

Card cert lookup

When you look up a graded card, Text Grab opens that grading company's website (PSA, CGC, TAG or Beckett) in Safari. For PSA, CGC and TAG the certification number is part of the web address, so it is sent to that company; for Beckett it is copied to your clipboard for you to paste into their search. This happens only when you tap to open the lookup, or if you turn on automatic opening in Settings. The photo itself is never sent. Those websites have their own privacy policies.

Data stored on your device

Your extraction history (the text you grab and a small thumbnail of each source image) is stored only on your device. It is never uploaded or synced to us or any third party. You can delete any entry, or all of them, at any time within the app; deleting the app removes it entirely.

Clipboard

When you copy text, it is placed on your device's system clipboard — standard iOS behaviour, entirely under your control. Text Grab does not monitor or send your clipboard anywhere.

Purchases

Text Grab Pro is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by Apple. We never see or receive your payment details. Whether you have purchased is checked directly with the App Store using your Apple Account; nothing about your purchase is stored on our side, because there is no server on our side. The count of free trial actions you have used is kept only on your device.

No data collection

We do not collect any data. There is no account to create, no email required, no usage analytics, no crash reporting, no third-party SDKs, and no advertising identifiers.

Children's privacy

Because Text Grab collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
