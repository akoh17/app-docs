Privacy Policy

VideoUploader · Last updated: 13 October 2026

VideoUploader is a personal publishing tool. It is run by the owner of the Facebook Pages "Peakpandafam" and "GridironAnime" and the Instagram accounts @peakpandafam and @gridironanime, on the owner's own computer, to publish the owner's own videos to the owner's own accounts. It is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and has no users other than the owner.

What the app accesses

When the owner connects their Facebook account, VideoUploader asks Meta for these permissions and uses each only as described:

PermissionUsed for
pages_show_listlisting the Facebook Pages the owner manages, so the owner can pick the Page to post to
pages_read_engagementreading the Page's basic details (name, ID) and the status of a posted Reel
pages_manage_postspublishing the owner's Reels to the owner's Page
instagram_basicreading the linked Instagram account's ID and username
instagram_content_publishpublishing the owner's Reels to the owner's Instagram account
business_managementfinding the Instagram account linked to the owner's Page

It reads nothing else: no messages, no comments, no followers, no friends, no insights, and no data about any other person.

Data stored on the owner's computer

Everything VideoUploader keeps stays in its data folder on the owner's own computer:

  • the login tokens Meta returns when the owner connects, so the owner does not have to log in for every post;
  • the Page and Instagram account the owner chose (ID and name);
  • a list of the owner's own posts: the video's file name, the caption, the time, and each post's status and link;
  • a copy of each video the owner uploads, until it is posted.

There are no servers on our side and no copy is kept anywhere else. Videos and captions are sent only to Meta's official Graph API, to be published on the owner's accounts.

No data collection

VideoUploader does not collect, read, store, sell, rent or share data about anyone other than the owner — not viewers, followers, or anyone who comments on or reacts to a post. There are no ads, no tracking cookies, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs. Nothing is shared with anyone except sending the owner's own posts to Meta to publish.

How long data is kept

Login tokens are kept until the owner disconnects or they expire (Meta's tokens last about 60 days). The post list is kept until the owner deletes entries in VideoUploader's History or deletes the data folder. Uploaded videos stay in the data folder until the owner deletes them.

Data deletion instructions

To remove all data VideoUploader holds:

  1. Open VideoUploader's Accounts page and press Disconnect. This deletes the stored login token.
  2. Remove the app's access in Facebook: Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations (or Apps and websites), find VideoUploader, and press Remove.
  3. Delete VideoUploader's data folder on the owner's computer. This deletes the post list and any uploaded videos.

Nothing else needs deleting, because nothing is kept anywhere else. Posts already published to Instagram or Facebook are controlled by those platforms: delete them there. To request deletion or ask any question about your data, email alexaoh123@gmail.com; we reply within 30 days.

Children's privacy

VideoUploader is not directed at children and is used by no one other than its owner.
