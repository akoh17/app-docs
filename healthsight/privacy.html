Privacy Policy

HealthSight · Last updated: 17 November 2026

HealthSight works out everything on your iPhone. Your health data never leaves your device, and HealthSight never writes to it. There is no account, no server of ours, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising. We never see your health data or anything you enter.

Health data

With your permission, HealthSight reads steps, walking and running distance, active energy, exercise minutes, flights climbed, sleep, resting heart rate, heart rate variability and workouts (their kind, date and length) from the Health app. You choose which types to share and can change this at any time in Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › HealthSight. Health data is used only to show you charts and findings inside the app and its widgets. It is never sent anywhere, never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with anyone.

What the app stores

Your tags, marked dates, guided tests, goals and settings are stored on your iPhone, in the app and in a shared area its widgets can read. A small summary of today's steps, last night's sleep and this week is kept there for the widgets. A copy of the last read from Apple Health is kept in the app's own storage so the app can open straight away. It's protected while your iPhone is locked, left out of backups, and deleted when you disconnect Apple Health in the app or delete the app. Deleting the app removes all of it.

Optional features that ask first

  • Weather (Pro): only if you tap "Add weather", HealthSight asks for your location, rounds it to about 10 km and sends that rough location and a range of dates to Apple's weather service to look up past daily weather. No health data is sent. Apple's privacy policy covers that service. Turn it off any time with "Stop using weather", which also deletes the saved weather and location.
  • Calendar (Pro, "Busy days"): only if you tap "Use my calendar", HealthSight reads when your events start and end, on your iPhone, to count how many hours each day was scheduled. It never reads event titles, people, places or notes, and nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Notifications: reminders you switch on yourself, scheduled on your iPhone. Nothing is sent through a server.

Purchases

HealthSight Pro subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or receive your payment details, and whether you have purchased is checked directly with the App Store using your Apple Account.

Sharing

A recap card, a report for your doctor or a spreadsheet leaves your iPhone only when you choose to share it, through the iOS share sheet, to the people and apps you pick.

No data collection

We do not collect any data. There is no account to create, no email required, no usage analytics, no crash reporting, no third-party SDKs and no advertising identifiers.

Children's privacy

Because HealthSight collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
