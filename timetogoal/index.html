Time to Goal

Time your routine. Beat your best.

Time to Goal is a timer for the routines you repeat: bed to out the door, a gym session, deep work. Start the timer when the routine starts, stop it when it ends, and the app keeps your time as the score: your best, your 7-day average, how you trend by week and by weekday, and what shaped each session.

What it does

  • Two kinds of goal — minimize time (get out the door faster) or maximize time (a longer gym session). The app knows which way is better for each.
  • The pace ring — while the timer runs you see where you are against your usual time and your best.
  • Start anywhere — the app, a Home Screen or Lock Screen widget, the Live Activity, Control Center, the Action Button, Siri, or a Shortcuts automation that starts the timer when your alarm stops.
  • Notes, feelings and factors — after a session, tap how it felt, which factors shaped it (prepped the night before, phone in bed) and a line of text, typed or dictated.
  • Trends — a chart per goal with your best and target, a weekday breakdown, and plain-language insights as your data grows.
  • Streaks that forgive — a week counts when you time the goal on enough of its days; two skips a week are free, and a broken streak is never shown as a loss.
  • Awards and cards — personal bests, barriers like Sub-20, totals, consistency and comebacks, with cards you can share.
  • Export — every session as CSV or a JSON backup, free, from Settings.

Common questions

Why didn't my session set a new best?

Only untouched timed sessions can set a best. A session you logged by hand, or whose times you edited, still counts for your averages and your streak, but it never sets a record. That keeps your bests honest.

How does the week streak work?

A week counts when you time the goal on enough of its scheduled days: daily needs 5 of 7, weekdays 4 of 5. The rest are skips. Missing more does not erase anything: your best streak stays and a new one starts the next week. Pause a goal from its menu when you are ill or travelling.

Can I turn the nudges off?

Yes: Me → Settings → Nudges. The app sends at most three per goal, each once: one for your second session, one if a second day slips, and one after a week away. Reminders are separate and per goal.

Where is my data?

On your device, and in your own iCloud account when sync is on. There is no account with us and no server of ours. Export everything from Settings whenever you like.
