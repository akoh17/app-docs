Privacy Policy

Time to Goal · Last updated: 6 November 2026

Time to Goal keeps your data on your device and, if you choose, in your own iCloud account. It has no account of its own, no servers, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising. We never see your sessions, notes or anything else you enter.

What the app stores

Your goals, timed sessions, feelings, factors, notes, streaks and awards are stored on your iPhone. If iCloud sync is on, the same data is stored in the private part of your iCloud account, which only your devices can read; Apple's privacy policy covers iCloud. You can delete any session or goal inside the app, export everything from Settings, and deleting the app (and its iCloud data from iCloud settings) removes it entirely.

What the app accesses, and why

  • Microphone and speech recognition — only while you dictate a note, and only after you allow it. Transcription runs on your device when your iPhone supports it; otherwise Apple's speech service processes the audio under Apple's terms. The audio is never stored or sent to us.
  • Notifications — local reminders and nudges that you can switch off in Settings. They are scheduled on your device; nothing is sent through a server.
  • Live Activities, widgets, Siri and Shortcuts — start and stop the timer from outside the app. They read the same data on your device.

Purchases

Time to Goal Pro and its free trial are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or receive your payment details, and whether you have purchased is checked directly with the App Store using your Apple Account. There is nothing on our side to store it.

Sharing

Cards you share (a new best, your week, a recap) leave the device only when you choose to share them, through the iOS share sheet, to the people and apps you pick. Notes are never on cards.

No data collection

We do not collect any data. There is no account to create, no email required, no usage analytics, no crash reporting, no third-party SDKs and no advertising identifiers. If a future version adds anonymous, aggregate usage statistics, this policy will say so first and it will be possible to turn off.

Children's privacy

Because Time to Goal collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
