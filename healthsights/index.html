HealthSights

Additional insights for your health tracking.

HealthSights reads the steps, sleep, heart and workout data you already track and turns it into patterns, trends and plain-English findings. Every finding says how many days it rests on and how sure it is.

What it does

  • Today against your usual: your steps so far against your own typical day for this weekday, and where you're on pace to finish.
  • Patterns: which day you move most, which hour carries your day, and whether you fade at the end of the month.
  • Trends: trend lines and averages for every measure, from one month to all time.
  • Tags and experiments: tag days with late caffeine, a late workout or anything else, and see what changes in your sleep and heart rate.
  • Week in review: a written summary of each week with one thing to try.
  • Widgets: today's pace on your Home Screen and Lock Screen.

Common questions

Why do I see no data, or only some?

HealthSights only shows what you've allowed it to read. Open Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › HealthSights and turn on the types you want. Some views need a few weeks of history before they say anything.

What do "Clear", "Likely" and "Could be chance" mean?

They compare a difference with how much your days normally vary. "Clear" is a large, steady gap; "Likely" is probably real; "Could be chance" means the gap is small next to how much your days already vary, so the app says so rather than guessing. Things that move together are shown as connections, never as causes.

How often does it update?

HealthSights opens on your last sync, syncs with Apple Health straight away, then every 30 minutes while it's on screen. It never syncs in the background. Today shows when it last synced; tap that line or pull down to sync now. Change the schedule in Settings › Apple Health sync.

Is this medical advice?

No. HealthSights summarises data you've recorded. It is not a medical device and doesn't diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. Talk to a doctor about any health concern.
